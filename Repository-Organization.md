# Repository Organization

## Content Folders
- `Chapters/` for ready manuscript chapters
- `Drafts/` for incomplete work
- `Research/` for source-backed notes
- `References/` for citation aids and source logs
- `Summaries/` for chapter or section summaries
- `Manuscripts/` for manuscript indexes and assembly helpers
- `Exports/` for generated output files

## Organization Rules
- Files tagged or labeled as research belong in `Research/`.
- Completed chapter drafts belong in `Chapters/`.
- In-progress chapter drafts belong in `Drafts/`.
- Summary files belong in `Summaries/`.
- Exported manuscript files belong in `Exports/`.

Use `python3 scripts/organize_files.py` to apply these rules automatically.
