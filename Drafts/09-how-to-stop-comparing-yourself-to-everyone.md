# How to Stop Comparing Yourself to Everyone

## Objective
Reduce the power of comparison by making its distortions visible and giving the reader more accurate mental models for what other developers are actually experiencing.

## Key Ideas
- Social media and public output hide struggle.
- Survivorship bias makes exceptional cases look normal.
- Comparison usually strips away context, timing, and constraints.

## Draft
Comparison feels honest.

That is why it is powerful.

You see another developer shipping, speaking, posting, explaining, or building, and your mind quietly turns their visible output into a full story about their internal state.

But you are not comparing whole lives.

You are comparing your internal confusion to someone else’s edited output.

That is not a fair benchmark.

It is a distortion.

Most developers are seeing fragments.

Not the failed drafts.

Not the uncertainty.

Not the recovery after mistakes.

REALITY CHECK:
Visibility is not the same thing as ease.

PATTERN:
See polished output → imagine total mastery → feel smaller → push harder without perspective
