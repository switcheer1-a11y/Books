# Rewriting the Internal Narrative

## Objective
Give the reader a simple recovery framework for turning automatic self-judgment into more accurate interpretation.

## Key Ideas
- Events are real, but interpretations are often distorted.
- Emotional debugging creates distance from identity panic.
- Rewriting thoughts requires accuracy, not forced positivity.

## Draft
The goal is not to think unrealistically positive thoughts.

The goal is to stop turning every event into an identity sentence.

A bug in production is an event.

"I am incompetent" is an interpretation.

Those are not the same thing.

One useful framework is this:

- Event
- Interpretation
- Distortion
- Rewrite

Event: I missed something important in review.

Interpretation: I am not good enough for this job.

Distortion: overgeneralization and catastrophizing.

Rewrite: I missed something important, which means I need a better review pattern, not a new identity.

DEBUG NOTE:
Rewriting is not denial. It is accurate recompilation.
