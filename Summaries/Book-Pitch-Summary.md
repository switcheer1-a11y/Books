# Book Pitch Summary

You’re Not a Fraud is a short nonfiction guide for developers who feel behind, overthink mistakes, and worry that everyone else understands more.

It explains why software work produces imposter syndrome so easily, why burnout and comparison intensify it, and how to build confidence from evidence instead of mood.

The tone is calm, rational, and practical.

The book is positioned as a professional mental performance guide for developers rather than generic self-help.
