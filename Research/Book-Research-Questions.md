# Book Research Questions

## Core Questions
- What research best explains imposter syndrome in knowledge work?
- What evidence supports the link between expertise and increased awareness of unknowns?
- What findings are useful on negativity bias, adaptation, and identity lag?
- What research best explains burnout cycles in high-performance environments?
- What sources clarify social comparison, survivorship bias, and distorted self-evaluation?

## Evidence to Gather
- Studies or expert sources on cognitive distortions
- Clear definitions that support a developer-friendly explanation
- Data or commentary on software industry change velocity
- Sources on uncertainty tolerance and self-trust under pressure

Use this file to strengthen explanations without making the book sound academic or clinical.
